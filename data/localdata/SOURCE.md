# Source of these photos

400 photos (512×512) taken from the AgriFreshNET "Processed Data" release:

Risdin, F., Salauddin, H., Tufa, T. A., & Hossain, M. J. (2026). AgriFreshNET
Freshness and Shelf-Life Image Dataset (Version 1). Mendeley Data.
https://doi.org/10.17632/42m5tb7yv9.1 — Licence: CC BY 4.0.

Only original phone photos (`IMG_<date>_<time>_HDR*.jpg`) were used, so all
images come from the same camera and the same white-cloth setup. Augmented
copies (`aug_*`) and WhatsApp/other-named files were excluded. Photos were picked
evenly across each source folder and renamed `<crop>_<stage>_NNN.jpg`.
